Selected fields: literal, misc/freq, misc/grade, reading_meaning/rmgroup/reading
with r_type=ja_on or ja_kun, and English meaning elements.
No SKIP codes, query codes, dictionary indexing data, or name readings are included.

Selection: exactly the 1000 entries whose misc/freq ranks are 1 through 1000,
ordered by that rank. These ranks derive from four years of Mainichi Shimbun
word-frequency data, analysed by Alexandre Girardi. This is a newspaper-based
frequency ranking, not a claim about every register of Japanese or a live 2026
usage survey. The snapshot date is the dictionary build date, not the corpus date.

Adaptation: source Japanese readings preserved exactly, with multiple readings
joined by the separator ・. English meanings retained in original order and
joined with semicolons. Short Russian learning glosses translated from the
English entries for this application; they are summaries of main senses,
not exhaustive dictionary definitions. English originals remain available
for comparison. Russian glosses have not received external editorial review.

Derived dictionary metadata and Russian glosses: CC BY-SA 4.0.
The source dictionary remains copyright its original authors.
The application code is a separate work; this notice does not relicense code.

Update procedure: on a dictionary-data refresh, obtain the latest official
kanjidic2.xml.gz, record its header and SHA-256, select ranks 1–1000, preserve
ja_on/ja_kun and English meanings, review Russian glosses for changed entries,
revalidate glyph availability/counts against the pinned KanjiVG snapshot, and
rebuild kanji.js plus the standalone application. Update the snapshot metadata
and this notice on each refresh. No automatic network calls are made at runtime.
